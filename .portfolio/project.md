LuminAI teaches AI to students who would not pay for a course: CNNs, RNNs, OpenCV and NLP over six weeks, ending in a capstone. Cohorts run past 200 students across more than ten countries, and I mentored over 40 capstone teams. I built the LMS it runs on in Next.js, Supabase and Prisma, with self-paced lessons, live submission tracking and auto-grading, so instructors spend their time on teams instead of spreadsheets.
